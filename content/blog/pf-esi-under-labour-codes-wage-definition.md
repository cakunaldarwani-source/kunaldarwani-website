The four Labour Codes – on Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, and Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions – came into force on **21 November 2025**. They replace 29 central labour laws. For most employers the change that hits payroll first is the uniform definition of **"wages"**.

## Who must register

| Scheme | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Provident Fund (EPFO) | Establishments employing **20 or more** persons |
| Employees' State Insurance (ESIC) | Establishments employing **10 or more** persons, in notified areas |

Once covered, an establishment stays covered even if employee numbers later fall below the threshold.

## Contribution rates

| | Employee | Employer |
|---|---|---|
| Provident Fund (incl. EPS) | 12% of wages | 12% of wages (8.33% to EPS, subject to the wage ceiling) |
| ESI | 0.75% of gross wages | 3.25% of gross wages |

PF is compulsory for employees earning wages up to **₹15,000 per month**. Beyond that, coverage is voluntary but common. ESI covers employees with gross wages up to **₹21,000 per month** (₹25,000 for persons with disability).

ESI contributions are counted in two six-month periods, April to September and October to March, and an employee's eligibility for cash benefits is linked to the contributions paid in the corresponding period. Employees whose average daily wage is up to ₹176 do not pay the employee share, though the employer still pays its 3.25%.

## The 50% wage rule

Under the Codes, "wages" means basic pay, dearness allowance and retaining allowance. Certain components are excluded, such as HRA, conveyance, overtime, commission and the employer's PF contribution. **If the excluded components exceed 50% of total remuneration, the excess is added back to wages.**

Many Indian salary structures keep basic pay at 30–40% of CTC and pay the rest as allowances. After the Codes:

- the **PF base** may rise for employees whose PF is calculated on actual wages
- **gratuity** is computed on the larger wage figure
- **leave encashment, bonus and overtime** calculations change

The cost increase depends on how the structure is designed. Some employers keep take-home pay constant by restructuring CTC. Others absorb the higher employer contribution.

## Gratuity for fixed-term employees

Under the Code on Social Security, **fixed-term employees** become eligible for gratuity on a pro-rata basis after completing **one year** of service. Other employees still need five years of continuous service. Employers who rely on fixed-term contracts should provide for this liability in their accounts.

## Monthly compliance

- Pay PF and ESI contributions and file the **ECR by the 15th** of the following month.
- Generate a UAN for every new joinee, and keep KYC (Aadhaar, bank, PAN) linked.
- Interest and damages apply to delayed payments. The employee's share deducted but not deposited is treated very seriously.
- Also, the employee's share of PF and ESI is deductible for the employer only if it is deposited by the due date under the respective law.

## Documents usually needed for registration

- PAN of the establishment and proof of its constitution (incorporation certificate, partnership deed or LLP agreement)
- address proof of the premises and the date business started
- details of the employer, partners or directors, with identity proofs
- bank account details of the establishment
- a list of employees with dates of joining, wages and Aadhaar

Companies incorporated through SPICe+ get their EPFO and ESIC registration numbers along with incorporation. Other employers apply online on the respective portals.

## What we commonly see in practice

- **Salary structures with a low basic** and several allowances, designed to keep PF small, which now fail the 50% test.
- **Contract and outsourced workers left out** of the headcount when deciding whether the threshold has been crossed.
- **KYC mismatches** in name or date of birth between Aadhaar and EPFO records, which block transfers and withdrawals later.
- **ECRs filed a few days late** each month, building up interest and damages that surface only at an inspection.

## What to do now

1. Run your current salary structures through the 50% test.
2. Quantify the change in PF, gratuity and bonus cost.
3. Update appointment letters and policies, especially for fixed-term staff.
4. Track the state rules, which are being notified in stages.
