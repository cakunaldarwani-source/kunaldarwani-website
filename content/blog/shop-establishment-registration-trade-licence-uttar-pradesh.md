Business owners often mix up three different things: GST registration, Shop & Establishment registration and a trade licence. They serve different purposes and come from different authorities.

| | Law | Issued by | What it deals with |
|---|---|---|---|
| GST registration | CGST and UPGST Acts | GST department, online | Tax on supplies |
| Shop & Establishment | State shops and establishments law | Labour Department | Working hours, holidays, wages records |
| Trade licence | Municipal Act and bye-laws | Nagar Nigam or local body | Permission to run a trade at the premises |
| FSSAI registration or licence | Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 | Food safety authority | Food businesses only |

## Shop & Establishment registration

Each state has its own Shops and Establishments Act. In Uttar Pradesh, the law is the **Uttar Pradesh Dookan Aur Vanijya Adhishthan Adhiniyam, 1962**, administered by the Labour Department. It applies to shops and commercial establishments in the areas where it is notified, which include municipal areas such as Agra.

The law regulates:

- daily and weekly **working hours**, and opening and closing times
- **weekly holidays** and leave
- **overtime** payment
- employment of women at night, with safeguards
- maintenance of **registers** of employees, attendance and wages

Registration is made online on the Labour Department portal. Over the past few years, several states have relaxed their requirements: very small establishments with few or no employees may be exempt or may only need to file an intimation. The thresholds have changed more than once, so check the current notification for your establishment.

The Uttar Pradesh law was amended in 2025, and the amendment changed which establishments have to register. Confirm the current position on the Labour Department's portal before applying or deciding that you are exempt.

## Trade licence

A trade licence is a **municipal permission** to carry on a specific trade at a specific premises. It is issued by the Nagar Nigam or the local body under its bye-laws. It is typically needed for:

- eating places, sweet shops, bakeries and food processing
- businesses dealing in hazardous or inflammable materials
- trades that may cause noise, smell or public inconvenience
- certain commercial activities listed in the local body's schedule

Health and fire safety requirements are often checked at the time of the trade licence. A trade licence is not a substitute for an FSSAI licence for food businesses. The two are separate.

## Documents usually asked for

**For Shop & Establishment registration**

- identity and address proof of the proprietor, partners or directors
- PAN, and the constitution document for a firm, LLP or company
- proof of the premises: rent agreement or ownership proof
- the number of employees, with the name of the manager
- date of starting business and nature of business

**For a trade licence**

- ownership or rent documents for the premises, with the owner's no-objection where rented
- identity proof and photographs of the applicant
- a site plan or layout, where the local body asks for it
- fire safety clearance, for trades and premises where it is required
- the latest property tax receipt of the premises

## What we commonly see in practice

- **Name mismatches.** The trade name on the shop registration differs from the name on the GST certificate or bank KYC, and the bank rejects the document.
- **Old details never updated.** The registration still shows the first address or a smaller headcount years after the business has grown or moved.
- **Licence in the landlord's name.** A trade licence obtained by the property owner is assumed to cover the tenant's business.
- **Food businesses** relying on the trade licence alone without FSSAI registration, or the other way round.

## Why these registrations matter

- **Banks** ask for proof of business existence to open a current account for a proprietorship. Shop & Establishment registration is one of the most commonly accepted documents.
- **Labour inspections** check the registers and working conditions required under the Act.
- **Landlords, aggregators and corporate customers** increasingly ask for them during onboarding.

## Keeping them current

- Renew the trade licence before expiry. Many local bodies charge a penalty for late renewal.
- Update the registration when the number of employees, the manager, the address or the ownership changes.
- When you close or shift the business, file the closure intimation so that dues and notices do not continue to accrue.
