## Areas of practice

The firm works in eight practice areas:

- **Business setup** – companies, LLPs, partnership firms, proprietorships, start-ups, NGOs, NBFCs and foreign-owned entities
- **Registrations & licences** – GST, FSSAI, IEC, PF/ESI, Shop & Establishment, trademarks, ISO and product certifications
- **Accounting & outsourcing** – bookkeeping, payroll, and accounting services for overseas businesses and accounting firms
- **Taxation** – income tax, TDS and GST compliance, notices, assessments and appeals
- **Audit & assurance** – statutory, tax, internal and stock audits
- **Company law & ROC** – annual and event-based filings, conversions and closures
- **Finance & advisory** – project reports, CMA data, business structuring and valuation
- **Documentation** – business agreements and digital signature certificates

## Who we work with

Individuals and HUFs, proprietors, partnership firms, LLPs, private and public companies, trusts, societies and Section 8 companies, start-ups, and businesses and accounting firms outside India that need accounting support.

## Professional standards

The firm and its partners are bound by the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, the regulations made under it, and the Code of Ethics issued by ICAI, including its requirements on confidentiality, independence and professional competence.
