This policy explains how Kunal Darwani & Company, Chartered Accountants ("the firm"), handles personal information collected through this website.

## Information we collect

This website does not use advertising trackers, and it does not ask you to create an account. If you contact the firm by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details and the nature of your query.

The website's hosting provider may automatically log technical information, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

## How we use it

We use the information you share only to respond to your query and, if you engage the firm, to carry out the engagement. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not send unsolicited communications.

## Confidentiality

Information received from clients in the course of an engagement is kept confidential, as required by the Code of Ethics of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, and is disclosed only with consent or where the law requires it.

## Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask the firm to access, correct or erase personal information we hold about you, subject to legal retention requirements. Write to the email address on the Contact page.

## Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
