A TDS mismatch notice arises when the tax deducted at source claimed in your return differs from what appears in Form 26AS or the Annual Information Statement (AIS). The Centralised Processing Centre (CPC) allows credit only for TDS reported by the deductor. Any excess claim is cut, and a demand or reduced refund follows. Most mismatches are fixed by getting the deductor to correct its TDS statement and then asking for rectification. Where the issue is timing, a specific application shifts the credit to the right year.

References below are to the Income-tax Act, 1961, which governs years up to FY 2025-26. From tax year 2026-27, TDS provisions are consolidated in section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. For deductors, see our article on [TDS under section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025](tds-section-393-income-tax-act-2025-deductors.html).

## Why does a TDS mismatch happen?

| Cause | Who must act |
|---|---|
| Deductor has not filed the TDS statement for the quarter | Deductor files the statement |
| Wrong PAN quoted by the deductor | Deductor files a correction statement with the right PAN |
| TDS reported in a different quarter or year | Deductor corrects, or you apply under Form 71 / Form 102 |
| Tax deducted but not deposited | Deductor deposits; section 205 protects you |
| You claimed TDS on income not shown in the return | You correct the return |
| Same TDS claimed twice, or arithmetic error | You correct the return |

Salaried employees often see a mismatch when they changed jobs, when an employer revised a quarterly return late, or when Form 16 was issued before the TDS statement was filed.

## What does the CPC do with the difference?

While processing the return, the CPC computes tax and allows TDS credit as per Form 26AS. If you claimed more:

- The excess credit is disallowed, and the intimation under section 143(1) shows a demand or a lower refund.
- Where the return was correct and the deductor was at fault, the demand stays until the deductor's correction reflects in Form 26AS and the return is rectified.

Separately, income appearing in the AIS but missing from the return can lead to a verification letter on the Compliance Portal.

## Step 1: Compare the three documents

Download Form 26AS, the AIS and your Form 16 or Form 16A. List every TDS entry by deductor, TAN, amount and year. The difference will usually point to one deductor or one quarter.

## Step 2: Ask the deductor to correct its TDS statement

Give the deductor your PAN, the amount, the date of deduction and the quarter. Ask them to file a correction statement and to share the confirmation. Only the deductor can change what it has reported.

## Step 3: Request rectification

Once the corrected credit appears in Form 26AS, file an online rectification request under section 154 on the e-filing portal, choosing the option for tax credit mismatch correction. The CPC then reprocesses the return with the revised credit.

If the demand has already been raised, respond to it on the portal under **Pending Actions > Response to Outstanding Demand**, stating that it is disputed and why. This helps avoid the refund of a later year being adjusted against it.

## What if TDS was deducted in a later year than the income?

This happens when income is accounted for on an accrual basis but the payer deducts TDS only when it actually pays. The general rule is that TDS credit is given in the year in which the income is assessable.

Section 155(20) of the 1961 Act lets the taxpayer apply for the credit to be allowed in the earlier year in which the income was offered to tax. The application is made in **Form 71**, renamed **Form 102** under the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (section 288(1) of the 2025 Act). It must be filed within **two years from the end of the financial year in which the TDS was deducted**.

## What if the employer deducted tax but never deposited it?

Section 205 provides that the person from whose income tax was deducted cannot be called upon to pay that tax again to the extent of the deduction. Keep salary slips, Form 16 and any correspondence with the employer as proof. Respond to the demand with this evidence, and report the deductor's default through the portal's grievance facility if needed.

## How do you reply to a TDS mismatch notice?

- **Proposed adjustment or demand from the CPC:** respond on the e-filing portal, attaching Form 16/16A and the deductor's confirmation of the correction.
- **Verification letter about AIS income:** respond on the Compliance Portal, giving feedback on each item.
- **Your own error:** revise the return if the window is open. For AY 2026-27, that is up to 31 March 2027, with a fee under section 234-I for later revisions.
