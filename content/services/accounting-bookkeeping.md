## Overview

Accurate books are the foundation for every tax return, audit, loan application and business decision. The firm maintains accounts on an ongoing basis, monthly or quarterly. We close each period with reconciliations so that GST returns, TDS statements and the books agree.

For companies, the accounting software must keep an **audit trail (edit log)** of every transaction, which cannot be disabled. We help clients set up and maintain this as the Companies (Accounts) Rules require.

## Who needs regular bookkeeping

Every company and LLP must maintain books of account, and so must businesses and professionals whose income or turnover crosses the limits set under the income tax law. Beyond the legal requirement, banks ask for current financial statements when renewing working capital limits, and GST and TDS returns are only as accurate as the books behind them. Companies must also preserve their books, with the audit trail, for the period the Companies Act prescribes, so records have to stay retrievable long after the year is closed.

## How the engagement works

1. We review the existing books and the chart of accounts and clean up where needed.
2. We agree a monthly calendar for sharing documents and closing the books.
3. We post transactions, reconcile, and deliver MIS reports by an agreed date.
4. At year end we prepare the financial statements and hand them over cleanly to the auditor.

## Issues we commonly see

- Suspense entries and unreconciled bank lines carried forward for months.
- Purchase bills recorded in the books but missing from GSTR-2B, or the other way round, so the credit claimed does not match the books.
- TDS deducted by customers that never reaches the receivables ledger, leaving balances overstated.
- Capital items expensed, or revenue expenses capitalised, which distorts both profit and depreciation.

## What to keep ready

A backup of, or access to, the existing accounting software, a list of all bank and loan accounts, the GST and TDS portal logins, and the names of the people who approve payments.
