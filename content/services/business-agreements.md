## Overview

Business agreements decide who bears which risk, how money flows and what happens when the relationship ends. They also have tax and stamp duty consequences that are often overlooked. Examples are the deductibility of partner remuneration, TDS on royalty and fees, and GST on franchise fees.

The firm drafts and reviews commercial agreements with the financial, tax and compliance terms built in. For matters involving litigation, title disputes or formal legal opinions, we work with advocates.

## When an agreement needs a closer look

- Starting a partnership or LLP, or admitting or retiring a partner.
- Bringing in an investor or co-founder, where control, exit and transfer of shares must be settled in writing.
- Renting premises, where the lease term affects both the stamp duty and whether registration is compulsory.
- Engaging consultants or franchisees, where the TDS and GST treatment depends on how the payment is described.

## How the engagement works

1. We discuss the commercial arrangement and identify the tax and regulatory points.
2. We prepare a draft for the parties' review and incorporate their comments.
3. We compute the stamp duty and arrange e-stamping, execution and, where required, registration.
4. We update your compliance records for any filing the agreement triggers, such as LLP Form 3 or GST registration amendments.

## Points we commonly see missed

Partnership deeds that do not authorise or quantify partners' remuneration and interest, which affects the firm's deduction under the income tax law. Agreements signed on plain paper or under-stamped, which causes difficulty if they ever have to be produced as evidence. LLP agreements changed in practice but never updated with the Registrar. Leave-and-licence agreements whose address details do not match the ones used for GST registration.
