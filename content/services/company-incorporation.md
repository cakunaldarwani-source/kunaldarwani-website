## Overview

A company is a separate legal person. It owns its assets, signs its own contracts and continues irrespective of changes in its shareholders. Its shareholders' liability is limited to the unpaid amount on their shares. These features make the company the usual choice for businesses that plan to raise equity, bring in investors or grow beyond a family-run setup.

The firm handles incorporation of every company form recognised under the Companies Act, 2013:

- **Private limited company** – minimum two directors and two shareholders, maximum 200 members
- **One Person Company (OPC)** – a single member with a nominee; NRIs may now also form an OPC
- **Public limited company** – minimum three directors and seven members
- **Section 8 company** – for charitable, educational, social or similar objects, with no distribution of profit
- **Nidhi company** – a mutual benefit company for borrowing and lending among members
- **Producer company** – formed by farmers or producer institutions

## How the engagement works

1. We discuss the business plan, who will hold shares, who will be directors, and whether a company is the right structure at all.
2. We check name availability against existing companies, LLPs and registered trademarks before filing.
3. We draft the MoA and AoA to suit the business, including share transfer restrictions where needed.
4. We file the SPICe+ forms with the linked registrations and track the application until the Certificate of Incorporation is issued.
5. After incorporation we complete the first-year steps: opening the bank account, receiving share capital, filing INC-20A, appointing the first auditor and setting up statutory registers.
