## Overview

A Digital Signature Certificate is the electronic equivalent of a signature, issued by a **Certifying Authority** licensed by the Controller of Certifying Authorities. It is required for MCA filings, for companies' and LLPs' income tax and GST returns, for e-tenders, for DGFT applications and for signing documents electronically.

## Who needs a DSC

- Directors and designated partners, for signing MCA forms and the annual filings of their company or LLP.
- Companies and LLPs, for returns that must be digitally signed.
- Bidders on government e-procurement portals, who usually need a signing and encryption certificate.
- Importers and exporters using the DGFT portal.
- Professionals who certify forms filed with MCA, income tax or GST.

## How the engagement works

1. We identify the right certificate type and validity for your use.
2. We submit the application to a licensed Certifying Authority and help with video and Aadhaar verification.
3. We register the DSC on the portals where you will use it, and track its expiry.

## Common issues we see

- A DSC that expires shortly before a filing deadline, leaving no time to obtain and register a new one.
- The name on the certificate not matching the PAN exactly, so the portal rejects registration.
- A new certificate obtained on renewal but not associated again with the director's profile on MCA, or not registered again on the income tax and GST portals.
- The USB token, its password or its driver software lost, or not working on a new computer.
- Organisation certificates issued in the name of an employee who later leaves, so the business loses access.

## Keeping the DSC usable

We keep a register of each certificate, its holder, type and expiry date, and start renewal well before expiry so that no filing is held up.
