## Overview

Businesses and accounting practices abroad face a shortage of qualified bookkeepers and rising costs. The firm provides an India-based team, supervised by Chartered Accountants, that works directly in the client's cloud accounting system during hours agreed to overlap with the client's working day.

Our role is **accounting and reporting support**. Tax returns, filings and attestations in the client's country are signed and filed by the client's own licensed professional, such as a CPA, chartered accountant, enrolled agent or registered tax agent. We prepare the workpapers that make their review quick.

## Engagement models

- **Dedicated accountant** – a named team member assigned to the client for a fixed number of hours each month.
- **Monthly close package** – a defined scope of bookkeeping, reconciliations and reports, delivered by a fixed date each month.
- **Project basis** – catch-up books, clean-ups, software migration or year-end support.

## How the engagement works

1. **Discovery call** – we understand the business, the software, the volume of transactions and the reporting needs.
2. **Engagement letter and NDA** – we define scope, timelines, access rights and data-handling terms.
3. **Onboarding** – we take user-level access (no shared passwords) and document processes in a client-specific SOP.
4. **Parallel month** – we close one month alongside the client or their previous bookkeeper to confirm the process.
5. **Steady state** – we close each month to a checklist, with CA review before reports are released, and hold a monthly review call.

## Data security

- Work is done only within the client's systems. Client data is not downloaded to personal devices.
- Each team member uses individual logins with multi-factor authentication. Access is removed immediately when a person leaves the engagement.
- Confidentiality obligations are covered in employment contracts and the client NDA.
- Personal data is handled in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the client's own data protection obligations.
