## Overview

Every Food Business Operator, from a home-based seller to a large manufacturer, needs either an FSSAI registration or a licence before starting operations. The category depends on turnover, the type of activity and the scale of production. Some activities, such as import and e-commerce, always need a Central licence.

## Which licence applies

FSSAI revised the turnover thresholds with effect from 1 April 2026:

| Category | Annual turnover |
|---|---|
| Basic registration | Up to ₹1.5 crore |
| State licence | Above ₹1.5 crore and up to ₹50 crore |
| Central licence | Above ₹50 crore |

Turnover is only one test. The activity, production capacity and location also matter. A registration or licence can be taken for a period of one to five years, and licensed manufacturers and importers file the annual return in Form D-1 by 31 May each year.

## How the engagement works

1. We map each premises and activity to the correct category, since one business can need different licences for different locations.
2. We prepare and file the application on FoSCoS with the correct food product categories, which affects what you are legally allowed to sell.
3. We handle inspection-related queries and the issue of the licence.
4. We track renewal and annual return deadlines so the licence never lapses.

## Common issues we see

- Food product categories left out of the application, so a product actually sold is not covered.
- The premises address on the application not matching the possession document.
- A licence lapsing because the renewal was not filed before expiry.
- One licence used for several kitchens or warehouses that each need their own.
