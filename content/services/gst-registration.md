## Overview

GST registration is the basis for charging GST, claiming input tax credit and dealing with registered businesses that expect tax invoices. Some businesses must register whatever their turnover, for example those making inter-state supplies of goods or selling goods through e-commerce operators. Others register voluntarily so that their customers can claim credit.

Since 2025, applications go through Aadhaar authentication, biometric verification at GST Seva Kendras in many states, and closer scrutiny of the place of business. A carefully prepared application avoids clarification notices and rejection.
