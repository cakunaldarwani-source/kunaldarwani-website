## Overview

GST compliance has become more automated and less forgiving. GSTR-3B liability is now auto-populated and locked from GSTR-1. Input tax credit is driven by the Invoice Management System. Returns cannot be filed once three years have passed from their due date. Monthly discipline in reconciling sales and purchases is the only reliable way to avoid blocked credit and demand notices.

## How the engagement works

1. We collect sales and purchase data each month by an agreed date.
2. We reconcile sales with e-invoices and GSTR-1, and purchases with IMS and GSTR-2B.
3. We action IMS entries, file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, and report mismatches and vendor follow-ups to you.
4. At year end we prepare GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C, reconciled with the audited financial statements.
