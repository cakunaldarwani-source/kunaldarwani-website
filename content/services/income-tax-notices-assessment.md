## Overview

Most income tax proceedings are now conducted **faceless** through the e-filing portal. There is no in-person meeting with an officer. The outcome depends on written submissions and evidence uploaded within tight time limits. A reply that misses the point, or a deadline that is missed, can turn a routine query into a high-pitched assessment.

Before replying, we check whether the notice itself is valid: whether it carries a valid DIN, was issued by the correct authority, and is within the time limit. Then we prepare a complete, evidence-backed submission.

## How the engagement works

1. We review the notice and the return, and identify exactly what is being asked and why.
2. We assess validity and limitation, and flag any legal objection.
3. We collect evidence and prepare the submission and annexures.
4. We file within time, track the proceedings, and evaluate the order for rectification or appeal.
