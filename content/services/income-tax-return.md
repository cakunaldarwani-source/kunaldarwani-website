## Overview

The return of income is the main document the Income Tax Department uses to assess you. With data now flowing in automatically from banks, brokers, employers and registrars into the **Annual Information Statement (AIS)**, a mismatch between the return and the AIS is a frequent trigger for notices. The firm prepares returns after reconciling every item of income against the department's data.

The **Income-tax Act, 2025** applies from 1 April 2026, starting with **tax year 2026-27**. Returns for FY 2025-26, filed during 2026, are still governed by the 1961 Act. We handle both.

## How the engagement works

1. We collect documents and pull the AIS, TIS and Form 26AS.
2. We compute income under both regimes where an option exists, and recommend one.
3. We share a draft computation for your approval, then file and e-verify the return.
4. We track processing, refunds and any intimation, and plan advance tax for the next year.
