## Overview

A brand name, logo, software or design is often a business's most valuable asset. Registering it creates enforceable rights and makes it easier to license, franchise or sell.

The firm assists with the business side of intellectual property: searching, preparing applications, maintaining records and treating IP correctly in accounts and tax. Representation before the Trade Marks Registry and the Patent Office in objections, oppositions and hearings is handled with **registered trademark and patent agents or advocates**, as the law requires.

## How the engagement works

1. We run a search to check that the mark is available and distinctive, and identify the correct classes.
2. We prepare the application and supporting documents and coordinate the filing.
3. We track examination and publication. If an objection or opposition arises, we coordinate the reply with a registered agent or advocate.
4. We maintain renewal dates and advise on royalty arrangements, IP valuation and their tax treatment.
