## Overview

An **internal audit** looks at whether a business's processes and controls work as intended, so that problems are found before they become losses or audit qualifications. Certain companies must appoint an internal auditor under Section 138 of the Companies Act. Many others appoint one voluntarily.

A **stock audit** is commissioned by a bank to verify the inventory and receivables against which working capital limits are given, and to check the **drawing power**.

## How the engagement works

1. We agree the scope and a risk-based audit plan with management or the bank.
2. We carry out fieldwork – process walk-throughs, sample testing and physical verification.
3. We discuss observations with process owners and agree corrective actions.
4. We issue the report, and follow up on the actions in the next cycle.
