## Overview

ISO standards describe how an organisation should manage quality, safety, information security and other risks. Customers, tender documents and export buyers increasingly ask for certification.

The firm helps organisations **implement** the standard and get ready for certification. The certificate itself is issued by an independent **accredited certification body** after its own audit. The firm does not issue certificates. Choosing a certification body accredited by NABCB or another IAF member makes the certificate widely acceptable.

## How the engagement works

1. **Gap assessment** – we compare current practices with the standard's clauses.
2. **Documentation** – we draft the policies, procedures and records the standard requires, kept to what the business will actually use.
3. **Implementation** – we run awareness sessions, and the records are kept for a period before the audit.
4. **Internal audit and management review** – both are mandatory before certification.
5. **Certification audit** – we coordinate with the certification body for Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits and support closure of any non-conformities.
