## Overview

Foreign companies entering India usually set up a **wholly owned subsidiary** or a **joint venture** with an Indian partner. Most sectors allow 100% foreign investment under the automatic route. Some sectors have caps, need government approval, or are prohibited. Investment from countries sharing a land border with India always needs government approval under Press Note 3 (2020).

Indian businesses investing abroad, by setting up a foreign subsidiary or joint venture, follow the Overseas Investment Rules, 2022, with their own filings and limits.

## How the engagement works

1. We confirm the entry route, any sectoral conditions, and the right type of Indian entity.
2. We incorporate the entity and coordinate the receipt of funds through banking channels.
3. We obtain the valuation, allot shares and file FC-GPR within 30 days of allotment.
4. We maintain annual FEMA compliance, including the FLA return, and review transfer pricing and withholding tax on payments to the parent.
