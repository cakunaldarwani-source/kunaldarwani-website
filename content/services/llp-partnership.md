## Overview

Partnerships suit professionals, traders and family businesses where a few people share ownership and management. There are two forms.

**A Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)** is a body corporate registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Partners' liability is limited to their agreed contribution, and the LLP continues even if partners change. Every LLP needs at least two designated partners, and at least one of them must be resident in India.

**A partnership firm** is formed by a deed between partners. Registration with the Registrar of Firms is optional under the Partnership Act. However, an unregistered firm cannot sue third parties to enforce contracts. In a partnership firm, partners have unlimited liability, both jointly and individually.
