## Overview

Organisations working in education, health, relief of the poor, environment and other charitable purposes can be set up as a **public charitable trust**, a **society** or a **Section 8 company**. Each has a different governance structure, a different level of regulatory oversight, and different acceptability with donors and CSR funders.

Registration of the entity is only the first step. To keep its income exempt from tax, and to let donors claim a deduction, the organisation must also register under the Income-tax Act. It must then keep that registration valid by filing returns, getting its accounts audited and applying its income to its objects.

## How the engagement works

1. We discuss the objects, the founders' role in governance and the expected sources of funds, and recommend the right structure.
2. We draft the constitutional document with clear charitable objects, so that it does not cause trouble during income tax registration.
3. We complete entity registration and then the provisional and regular income tax registrations.
4. We set up accounting, audit and the annual return calendar, along with the NGO Darpan, CSR-1 and FCRA requirements where they apply.
