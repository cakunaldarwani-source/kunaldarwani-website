## Overview

Payroll combines tax, labour law and confidentiality. Errors show up as employee grievances, interest and penalties on TDS, and PF and ESI demands. The firm runs payroll each month to a fixed calendar, handles every statutory deduction, and issues the reports employees and management need.

## How the engagement works

1. We set up the employee master and salary structures and review them against the Labour Codes.
2. Each month we process attendance inputs, compute salaries and deductions, and share payslips and the bank file for approval.
3. We deposit TDS, PF and ESI and file the related returns.
4. At year end we issue TDS certificates and reconcile salary figures with the books.
