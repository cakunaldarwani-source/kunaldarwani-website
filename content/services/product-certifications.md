## Overview

Many products cannot legally be sold in India, or exported, without a product-specific certification. Some services, such as private security, need a sector licence. Requirements depend on the product, the market and the regulator, and they change as new Quality Control Orders are notified.

The firm helps clients identify what applies, prepares the documentation, and coordinates with accredited labs and certification bodies. Certificates and licences are issued by the respective authorities and bodies.

## How the engagement works

1. We map the products and markets to the mandatory and voluntary certifications.
2. We prepare technical files, process documentation and applications.
3. We coordinate testing, inspection and audits.
4. We maintain renewals and surveillance requirements.
