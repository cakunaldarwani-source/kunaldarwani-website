## Overview

Banks lend on the strength of a clear, credible financial case. The firm prepares project reports and CMA data that set out the project cost, the means of finance, the projected profitability and the borrower's repayment capacity, using assumptions that can be defended in a credit appraisal.

The decision to sanction a loan rests entirely with the lender. The firm prepares and certifies financial information. It does not arrange or guarantee loans and does not act as a loan agent.

## How the engagement works

1. We understand the project, the funding need and the lender's format requirements.
2. We build the financial model – costs, revenue, working capital and the repayment schedule.
3. We prepare the report or CMA data, and the certificates the lender asks for.
4. We help answer the lender's appraisal queries on the numbers.
