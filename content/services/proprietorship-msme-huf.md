## Overview

A **sole proprietorship** is the simplest way to start: the business and the owner are the same person for law and tax. There is no single registration that creates a proprietorship. It is identified by its registrations, such as GST, Udyam, Shop & Establishment and the trade licence. Banks usually ask for two of these to open a current account in the business name.

**Udyam registration** gives a business MSME status. It is free and paperless and is based on PAN and Aadhaar. With it, the business can access priority-sector lending, collateral-free credit under CGTMSE, government tender benefits and protection against delayed payments.

A **Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)** is a separate taxpayer with its own PAN and basic exemption. Where family members inherit ancestral property, receive gifts for the family, or want to hold family assets in one place, an HUF can be useful for tax planning and succession.
