## Overview

The Companies Act requires companies to file their financial statements and annual return every year, and to report specified events within set time limits. Late filing attracts an additional fee for each day of delay, with no upper cap for some forms. Prolonged non-filing leads to directors being disqualified and to the company being struck off.

## How the engagement works

1. We prepare a compliance calendar for the company based on its AGM date and its events.
2. We draft Board and general meeting documents and maintain the minutes and registers.
3. We prepare and file the forms on the MCA V3 portal and track approvals.
4. We send reminders for periodic filings such as DIR-3 KYC, DPT-3 and MSME-1.
