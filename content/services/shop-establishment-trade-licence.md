## Overview

Shops, offices, restaurants and other commercial establishments come under their **state's Shops and Establishments law**. It regulates working hours, holidays, leave and the employment conditions of staff. Many establishments must also hold a **trade licence** from the municipal body. This applies especially to food businesses, businesses dealing in hazardous goods, and businesses that may be a public nuisance.

These registrations also serve as proof of business existence for opening a current account and for other registrations.

## How the engagement works

1. We check which registrations apply to each premises.
2. We file the online applications and track them until certificates are issued.
3. We maintain renewal dates and file amendments when staff numbers, address or ownership change.
