## Overview

DPIIT recognition gives a start-up access to tax and regulatory benefits: an income tax holiday on application, easier public procurement norms, a rebate on patent and trademark fees, faster winding up, and eligibility for government seed and fund-of-funds schemes.

Under the framework notified on 4 February 2026, an entity qualifies if it is:

- a private limited company, LLP, registered partnership firm or co-operative society
- **up to 10 years old** from incorporation, or **20 years** for deep tech start-ups
- below a turnover of **₹200 crore** in every year, or **₹300 crore** for deep tech start-ups
- working towards innovation or improvement of products, processes or services, or a scalable business model with potential for employment or wealth creation

An entity formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business does not qualify.

## How the engagement works

1. We assess eligibility and whether the deep tech category applies.
2. We prepare the application, with a clear statement of the problem, the solution and what is innovative about it.
3. After recognition, we evaluate the income tax holiday and file the Inter-Ministerial Board application if it is worthwhile.
4. We keep the start-up's compliance in order so that the benefits are not lost on review.
