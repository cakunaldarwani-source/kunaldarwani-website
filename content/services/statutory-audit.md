## Overview

Every company registered in India must have its financial statements audited each year by a Chartered Accountant in practice, whatever its size or turnover. LLPs, trusts, societies and co-operatives require audits when they cross the thresholds in their respective laws.

The auditor gives an independent opinion on whether the financial statements show a true and fair view. The auditor also reports on matters specified in the Companies Act and in CARO 2020. Engagements are accepted subject to the independence and other requirements of the ICAI Code of Ethics and the Companies Act.

## How the engagement works

1. Appointment is by the shareholders, or by the Board for the first auditor. We file our consent and eligibility certificate, and the company files ADT-1.
2. We plan the audit, understand the business, and assess risks and controls.
3. We carry out substantive procedures and obtain confirmations and management representations.
4. We report, and discuss observations with management and those charged with governance.
