## Overview

A tax audit is an audit of accounts required by the Income-tax Act when a business's turnover, or a professional's gross receipts, exceeds the prescribed limits. It also applies in certain presumptive taxation cases. The auditor reports on a long list of tax-specific matters in the prescribed report, and the department relies on this report in processing and scrutiny.

From tax year 2026-27, the provision is Section 63 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. It was Section 44AB under the 1961 Act.

## How the engagement works

1. We confirm whether the audit applies, including the cash-transaction condition for the higher limit.
2. We review the books and the tax positions, and reconcile them with GST and TDS data.
3. We prepare the report and discuss reportable items with you before finalising.
4. We upload the report and coordinate the filing of the return by its due date.
