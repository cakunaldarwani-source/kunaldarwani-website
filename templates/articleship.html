What do articled assistants work on?

Articled assistants work on live assignments under the supervision of the partners, CA. Kunal Darwani (FCA, DISA) and CA. Reet Darwani (FCA, M.Com). Three areas form a large part of the training:

  • Statutory audits – audits of companies under the Companies Act, 2013, from planning and vouching to drafting the audit report and CARO, along with tax audits.
  • Bank audits – bank branch statutory audits during the audit season, and stock and internal audits for borrowers.
  • Litigation – income tax and GST notices, assessments and appeals: reading the notice, preparing submissions and paper books, and attending hearings with the partners.

Alongside these, articled assistants handle income tax and TDS returns, GST returns and reconciliations, ROC filings for companies and LLPs, and accounting, including work for businesses outside India.

How does the training work day to day?

  • End to end: an articled assistant follows an assignment from the client's first documents to the final filing, report or reply, rather than doing one isolated step.
  • Working and learning with AI: the firm uses AI tools in research, drafting, reconciliations and audit documentation. Articled assistants learn to use them in day-to-day work, and to check every output against the law and the client's records before it is used.
  • Review and feedback: work is reviewed by the partners, so assistants see how a file is corrected and why.

How is articleship structured under the ICAI New Scheme?

PointPosition under ICAI New Scheme
Duration2 years of practical training
When you can startAfter passing both groups of CA Intermediate and completing ICITSS
Leave12 days in each year of training
Industrial trainingOptional, 9 to 12 months, in the last part of the 2 years
RegistrationOnline, through the ICAI Self-Service Portal (SSP)
Minimum stipendPrescribed by ICAI under Regulation 48 of the Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1988

Who can apply?

  • CA students who have cleared both groups of CA Intermediate and completed ICITSS
  • Students seeking a transfer of articleship to Agra, subject to ICAI's transfer rules
  • CA Intermediate students who want to apply in advance of their results

How do I apply?

  1. Fill in the application form on the Careers page and select Article Assistant (CA student).
  2. Attach your resume (PDF or Word) with your CA Intermediate result details.
  3. Shortlisted applicants are called for a conversation at the office, after which the articleship is registered on the ICAI portal.

You can also email your resume to {{ site.email }} with "Articleship" in the subject line.
